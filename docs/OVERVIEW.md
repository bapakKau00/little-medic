# Little Medic: Game Overview

*A first-aid learning game for children, for teachers, parents and assessors.*

## What is Little Medic?

Little Medic is a colourful arcade-style game that teaches children **basic first aid**. Children learn:
- what the items in a first-aid kit are called;
- what each item is used for;
- how to look after a small wound, **step by step and in the right order**.

It plays in a web browser on a computer, tablet, phone or classroom projector, with nothing to install. Everything can be shown in **Bahasa Melayu or English**, and you can switch at any time with the **EN / BM** button.

## Who is it for?

- **Children:** primary-school age (about 7–12 years old).
- **Teachers:** for a health-education lesson, either played by students or led from the front on a projector.
- **Parents:** for learning at home.

## What children will learn

By the end of the game, children should be able to:
1. **Name** common first-aid items: gloves, clean water, gauze, antiseptic (iodine), plaster, elastic bandage, triangular bandage, scissors and cotton wool.
2. **Explain** what each item is used for.
3. **Treat a small wound in the right order:** put on gloves → wash with clean water → pat dry with gauze → apply antiseptic → cover with a plaster.
4. **Remember safety habits:**
   - always tell an adult when someone is hurt;
   - keep wounds clean;
   - mice and dirt carry germs.

## How the game works

### The journey
1. **Homepage:** press **PRESS START** and choose **MULA PERMAINAN** (Start Game). *How to Play* and *Settings* (sound, music, language) are also here.
2. **The Lobby:** three doors. Moving the mouse over a door opens it to give a peek of the room inside. Clicking it walks through.
3. **The three rooms**, described below.

### Room 1: Pukulan Kilat (*Adakah anda tangkas?* / Are you quick?)
**A fast, fun whack-a-mole game.**
- First-aid items pop up out of holes in an arcade machine, and so do some naughty mice.
- Children use a hammer to **hit the first-aid items (+10 points)** and **avoid the mice (−5 points)**, because mice carry germs.
- Hitting several items in a row gives a **combo** with extra points.
- There are three levels: **Mudah, Sederhana, Sukar** (Easy, Normal, Hard). A round lasts 45 seconds.
- At the end, children see their stars and score, plus a fun fact about one of the items they hit.

*What it teaches:* recognising first-aid items quickly, and their names.

### Room 2: Taman Permainan (*Bantu wira kecil!* / Help the little hero!)
**A short story where children treat a friend's wound.**
1. A short video shows a child riding a bicycle in a park who falls and hurts their knee: *"Aduh! Lutut saya terluka!"* ("Ouch! My knee is hurt!")
2. The child presses **BANTU KAWAN** (Help your friend), and the camera zooms in on the wound.
3. Using the **first-aid station**, children drag (or tap) the right item onto the wound, one step at a time:

| Step | Item | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gloves | Protects your hands and the wound from germs |
| 2 | Clean water | Washes away the dirt and blood |
| 3 | Gauze | Gently pats the wound dry |
| 4 | Antiseptic (iodine) | Kills germs |
| 5 | Plaster | Covers the wound to keep it clean |

- The wound **gets better after every correct step**, and the friend's face changes from crying to smiling.
- **Wrong item, or right item at the wrong time:** a friendly message explains why, for example *"Cotton wool can stick to a wound"*. Nobody "loses".
- At the end, children are reminded to **tell an adult**, and see a summary of the 5 steps.

*What it teaches:* the correct order of basic wound care, and why each step matters.

### Room 3: Kad Misteri (*Kenali, ingat dan fahami!* / Know, remember and understand!)
**A teacher-led review activity for the whole class.** There are no scores. It's about thinking together.
1. Eight mystery cards with a **"?"** are shown on the screen or projector.
2. The teacher clicks a card, and it flips to show a picture of an item.
3. Students **press their buzzers** if they know the item. The teacher presses **NAMA ITEM** (item name) to reveal the answer.
4. Students buzz again to say **what it is used for**. The teacher presses **FUNGSI ITEM** (item function) to show the explanation in big letters, and the class discusses it together.

*What it teaches:* remembering and understanding. It's a good way to wrap up the lesson.

## Tips for teachers

- **Suggested lesson flow** (about 30–40 minutes):
  1. **Room 1:** warm-up, 5–10 minutes. Students take turns, or play in pairs.
  2. **Room 2:** main activity, 10–15 minutes. Play it together on the projector and ask the class to choose each step before anyone clicks.
  3. **Room 3:** review, 10 minutes, with buzzers.
- **Projector:** in Room 3, press the **⛶** button for full screen.
- **Sound:** the **♪** button mutes everything. To turn off only the music, use *Settings → Music*.
- **Language:** switch between BM and English at any time, even mid-game. It's useful for bilingual classes.
- **Real-life reminder:** stress that children should **always tell an adult** when someone is hurt, and only use first-aid items with an adult's help.

## How to open the game

1. Open the game folder and double-click **`index.html`** (or open the website link, if it's hosted online).
2. The game opens in the web browser. Chrome or Edge works best.
3. Press **PRESS START**. The music starts after the first click.

*An internet connection is recommended so the special arcade fonts load. Without it the game still works, in a plain font.*

## Important note

Little Medic teaches **basic** first aid for **minor** injuries only, such as small cuts and scrapes. It does not replace proper first-aid training. For serious injuries, children should always get help from an adult straight away.
